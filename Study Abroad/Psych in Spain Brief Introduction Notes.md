- Psychology as a profession in Spain is recent (late 70s)
- High unemployment rate
- Mostly women working on the private sector


### Early Development
- Traced back to latter 19th century
- Santiago Ramon y Cajal dominated during the early period, considered one of the fathers of psychobiology. 
- Mostly used psychology for vocational guidance and industrial efficiency and rehab. 
- Developments from above vanished with the civil war and were replaced with scholastic psychology.

### Present Day
- Psychology became popular in the 70s and 80s due to the expanding student body
- Takes five years for a degree, requires PhD for research.

### Academic Organization
- Divided in faculties and departments
	- Faculties are like Colleges (of engineering, of sciences, etc)
- Psychology is divided into 6 subject areas
- Amount and quality of produced research has gone up in the past 20 years.
- Each concentration publishes their research in different places and has different requirements for promotion
- Psychobiology is clearly ahead of the rest in quality
- 'Colegio Oficial de Psicologos' is the main professional association
	- Accredits people, holds conferences, promotes popular interests

### Future Challenges
- Reduce the high unemployment rate
- Improve teaching conditions and curricula
- Raise research standards
